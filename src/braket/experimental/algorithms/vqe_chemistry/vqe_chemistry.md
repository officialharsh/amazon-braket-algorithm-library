An end-to-end Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) workflow for molecular ground-state and
binding (reaction) energies on Amazon Braket. Build a molecular Hamiltonian from atomic
coordinates with an active space, optimize a particle-conserving AllSinglesDoubles ansatz on
the local simulator, evaluate the converged energy on Amazon Braket SV1 or a QPU (IonQ Forte),
and combine fragment energies into a binding energy. Includes a real IonQ Forte hardware run
for H2 (raw and error-mitigated) as a hardware reality check.
